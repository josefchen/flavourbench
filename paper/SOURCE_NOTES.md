## Bias and robustness map

- Position bias: every judge/comparison repeats with swapped sides; inconsistent normalized
  choices are excluded.
- Self preference: a judge is excluded from primary analysis whenever its Season 0 model is
  present.
- Judge agreement: original/swapped consistency is reported in the paper; pairwise Cohen’s kappa is retained in the internal aggregate analysis.
- Verbosity: preference for the longer answer among unequal-length directional decisions is
  reported.
- Public-source contamination: exact 8- and 12-token hidden-reference overlap is calculated
  after subtracting question n-grams; a 12-token exclusion sensitivity is retained in the internal aggregate analysis.
- Infrastructure attribution: reconciled terminal failures, explicit provider rejection,
  and uncertain delivery are distinct.
- Comparison graph: historical global Bradley-Terry ratings are withheld unless all 12 endpoints
  are connected by valid non-both-bad comparisons. The new 16-endpoint candidate graph is
  connected, but no fit is reported before real ballots exist.
